# Paddy harvest animation

The homepage opens with a full-screen harvest animation, the published Farminix logo at the top, and the tagline **Family Choice Brand**. The scene uses a deep purple background, a light purple logo without a surrounding card, and the actual Farminix Family Choice Rice bag beneath the falling grains. The footer uses the same light purple logo treatment; the regular site header retains its original brand colors. The opening autoplays a complete ten-second harvest sequence before revealing the homepage. Completion comes from the animation itself, rather than a wall-clock dismissal timer. An **Enter Site** button is available immediately on desktop and mobile, including while artwork is loading. Clicking it closes the intro, clears any search/hash, and opens the main homepage at the top with focus on the main content. Pausing or switching away from the tab preserves the remaining animation; returning resumes it. It replays on a page reload but does not replay during internal navigation. The existing intro enabled setting controls it; the configured story videos remain available through Watch Our Story.

The same scene remains immediately below the main hero and above the product collection. Desktop uses native WebGL 2 with instanced geometry for paddy stems, narrow leaves, grain clusters, and individual falling grains. Phones and touch devices use a Canvas 2D renderer: the shaded plants are rasterized once, then each frame draws the cached plant and at most 24 rice sprites. Drawing is capped at 30 frames per second and 1.25 pixel density; the sequence follows active elapsed time so dropped frames do not stretch it into slow motion. Mobile omits the large blurred background photograph, animated SVG hierarchy, and product-image filters. The existing transparent `public/farminix_rice_front.png` is reused without modifying the product packaging.

Grains detach from their moving stem positions, accelerate downward, rotate around multiple axes, and move through perspective depth. Warm lighting gives the grains their rounded surface. The paddy sways gently; cursor movement changes the camera perspective.

Implementation:

- `src/components/PaddyHarvestScene.tsx`: controls, visibility handling, reduced-motion preferences, and fallback.
- `src/components/paddyHarvestRenderer.ts`: geometry, lighting, wind, grain release, gravity, and renderer cleanup.
- `src/components/paddyHarvestMobileRenderer.ts`: cached mobile plant, shaded grain sprites, frame-rate cap, real-time playback, and completion.
- `src/components/PaddyHarvestFallback.tsx`: final vector fallback when neither canvas renderer can initialize.
- `src/components/harvestTiming.ts`: full opening-sequence and falling-grain durations shared by the desktop and mobile renderers.
- `src/components/PaddyHarvestScene.css`: responsive layout, purple atmosphere, product placement, and vector animation.
- `src/components/IntroVideoOverlay.tsx` and its CSS: opening screen, logo, timing, and entry controls. A native modal dialog manages keyboard focus and prevents interacting with the page underneath; page scrolling is restored on close.
- `src/App.tsx`: lazy loading with the enabled homepage hero section.

The animation can be paused and resumed. It pauses when its visual stage is offscreen or when the document is hidden. Startup checks visibility directly after mounting and on scroll, resize, page return, and visibility changes, rather than depending solely on the intersection observer. Rendering is initialized only when the stage is visible; the below-hero scene stays suspended while the intro is open. Playback starts immediately without waiting for logo/product image downloads; images decode asynchronously. The opening plays automatically, including when reduced motion is enabled, as explicitly requested. Reduced motion uses the mobile renderer without stalk sway; the inline homepage scene still starts paused with a visible Play control. The vector fallback explicitly preserves real durations against the storefront's global reduced-motion rule. Desktop caps render pixel density and releases GPU resources on unmount. Browsers without WebGL 2 or that lose their graphics context automatically receive the cached canvas renderer, then the vector fallback if necessary. Both primary renderers report sequence completion directly. Enter Site remains available during loading or playback.

Validation covered real WebGL rendering, visible painted canvas pixels, moving and falling grains, pause/resume, full sequence completion, mobile layout, reduced-motion preferences, and entry navigation at 320, 390, 844, and 1440 pixels. The mobile canvas maintained about 30 frames per second in a local browser test with 4× CPU throttling. Delaying the product image confirmed that playback and Enter Site remain available during slow image loading; entry clears search/hash, restores scrolling, and focuses the homepage at scroll position zero. Internal navigation does not replay the intro. Earlier MP4 previews were recorded separately; the website runs the renderer directly and does not download those videos.
